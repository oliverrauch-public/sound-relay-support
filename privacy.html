Privacy Policy

Sound Relay Equalizer · Last updated November 10, 2026

Sound Relay Equalizer does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

Audio

Audio is processed on your device only and never leaves it. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no third-party services.

Permissions

The notification permission is used only to tell you when your headphones disconnect.

Settings

Your equalizer settings and presets are stored on your device only.
